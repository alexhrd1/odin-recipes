This is a recipe for a Hollandaise sauce. It is very nice and good.

Ingredients you'll need:

  • 2 Egg Yolks
  • 113g Butter
  • Half a Lemon

Instructions

  1. Melt butter in a saucepan on the stove
  2. Put egg yolks and lemon juice into a container just wider than the head of your immersion blender
  3. Once butter is bubbling hot, insert immersion blender into the container and get it running, then slowly drizzle in the butter.
  4. Continue slowly adding butter, moving the immersion blender up and down to emulsify
